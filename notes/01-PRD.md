# Product Requirements Document

## 1. Problem Statement

The Karnataka State Power Distribution Board currently identifies electrical faults manually after customer complaints. This process takes approximately two hours. The objective of this project is to reduce fault localization time to under two minutes by using IoT telemetry from pole-mounted devices.

---

## 2. Business Goals

- Reduce fault localization time
- Minimize false alarms
- Automatically verify restoration
- Improve operator confidence

---

## 3. Stakeholders

### Primary
- Control Room Operator

### Secondary
- Field Crew

### System
- Pole IoT Devices
